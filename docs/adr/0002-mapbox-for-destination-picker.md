# Mapbox for the Destination map picker, not Google Maps

The Destination map picker (pin-drop + reverse geocoding + address autocomplete) needs a map and geocoding provider. We considered Google Maps Platform (most accurate/complete global address data, but requires enabling billing even within its free tier) and Leaflet + OpenStreetMap/Nominatim (fully free, but rate-limited to 1 req/sec and unsuitable for this app's usage without self-hosting). We chose Mapbox: accurate enough for real-world addresses, a generous free tier with no billing-enablement friction, and a single access token to manage rather than a GCP billing account. Swapping providers later means replacing the SDK across the destination picker, the static-map thumbnail on trip detail, and the env var/token setup, so this is worth recording rather than rediscovering.
